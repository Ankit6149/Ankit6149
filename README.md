## How I think

I began with **Instrumentation & Control Engineering at NSUT**, where signals, feedback, sensing, and dynamic systems became the foundation of how I understand technology.

That curiosity was never limited to machines. I am equally drawn to the way **living systems sense, adapt, organise, communicate, and remain resilient**. It is the same reason I enjoy software, automation, applied AI, and interface design: each involves understanding many interacting parts and shaping them into something coherent.

At **Wyrd Media Labs**, I work across full-stack platforms, third-party integrations, workflow automation, tracking infrastructure, and applied AI. I like taking an ambiguous idea, understanding the system beneath it, and turning it into software that feels clear, dependable, and deliberate.

I care about both the internal logic and the final experience—the architecture, data flow, failure modes, visual composition, and the person using what I build.
